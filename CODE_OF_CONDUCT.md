# Code of Conduct

Tilecast contributors must make participation welcoming and harassment-free, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint. Be respectful, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and focus disagreement on the work. Basically, just be a good person.

Maintainers may remove comments, code, or participants whose behavior is threatening, discriminatory, harassing, or persistently disruptive. Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Maintainers will review reports promptly, preserve confidentiality where possible, and take proportionate action.
